# StudioSuits gap analysis: suits.me

Status: Draft for owner review. No site changes have been made from this report.
Audit date: October 26, 2026 (all live observations, America/New_York, desktop unless stated).
Prepared by: Boone, lead product UX auditor for suits.me.
Rule for this document: no claim appears here without a source. Anything that could not be checked is listed in the Unverified register (section 7), not filled in.

## 1. Scope

- Sites: https://www.suits.me/ (primary) and https://www.studiosuits.com/ (benchmark).
- Repository: suitsme-ak/suitsme-monorepo, main at commit 56b6f4d on the audit date.
- Categories in scope: SUITS, JACKETS, TROUSERS, SHIRTS, WEDDINGS, MORE, WOMEN, and every live submenu under them.
- Journey in scope: navigation, listing, product, fabric and variant, customization, size and measurements, bag or cart, checkout (stop before any real order), plus the experience after a user logs in.
- Boundaries kept: no real order placed on either site, no real customer data used, no login performed on either site, no payment details entered.

## 3. Owner decisions already applied

- D1: suits.me will move to real pages with real URLs, like StudioSuits, instead of one page with hash routes. NAV-01 stays a gap and becomes a headline work package in the implementation plan.
- D2: TROUSERS (StudioSuits uses PANTS) was deliberate. Not a gap. No row and no change.
- D3: Collection depth will be handled by a future catalog API integration. 36 designs per collection are accepted for now. Depth is not scored as a defect below.
- D4: The customization entry difference (3D studio vs step flow) is deprioritized. See CUS-05, rated P3 by owner direction.

## 4. Strengths to keep (verified, do not regress)

1. Guided body measurements on suits.me are better than the benchmark's visible flow: one field at a time with an illustration, written guidance per field, a working inches and cm toggle, and validation that blocks empty fields with an allowed range.
2. Price consistency on suits.me: the test suit was $399 on the listing, the product page, in the bag, and at checkout. StudioSuits showed the same suit at $299, $304, and $312 on three surfaces.
3. Honest labeling on suits.me: the product page discloses that the main image is an AI generated illustration matched to the cloth, and checkout openly calls itself a design preview and demo.
4. Fabric led product cards on suits.me: code, composition, pattern, and a real swatch on every card suits a fabric first shopper.
5. Fit preference (Slim, Tailored, Relaxed) is explained in plain words and kept separate from body measurements.

## 5. Benchmark flaws observed on StudioSuits (recorded so they are not copied)

- Same product priced $299 on the collection card, $304 on the product page and cart, $312 for the same fabric in the Custom Suit builder.
- Cart details list omits Pocket Accent and Monogram selections.
- Checkout newsletter checkbox is prechecked.
- A product rating renders as "4.9.5" text.
- The jacket size guide table runs to size 62 while the jacket size dropdown stops at 60.
- Quick Add and Load More behavior could not be confirmed in one attempt each. Recorded as unverified, not as defects.

## 7. Unverified register (not gaps, not guesses)

1. suits.me on mobile: the browser tooling had no viewport switch, so the mobile menu, any hamburger, and mobile submenu behavior are unverified. StudioSuits mobile was observed (hamburger labeled "Menu", two level expanding drawer) and is the reference when this is checked.

4. StudioSuits measurement form behind login: fields, guidance, and validation unverified. No login was performed, per the audit boundaries.
5. StudioSuits: whether an order can complete with measurements deferred, and whether measurements can be submitted after checkout: unverified.
6. StudioSuits menu destinations not individually verified: Jackets and Pants text links, Suits and Weddings Featured links, and whether the Weddings More column is empty or failed to render.
7. Garment specific option sets on suits.me beyond suits (jackets, trousers, shirts, womenswear customization) have not been walked. CUS-01 and CUS-02 are proven for suits and likely shared, but each garment must be confirmed before its fix is scoped.
8. suits.me keyboard and screen reader behavior for menus and the customizer: not tested in this pass.
9. Commerce test suite result: not judged, because dependencies were not installed locally. Catalog and suit-edits suites passed on the audit date.
